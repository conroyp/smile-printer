Why we’re doing this

A small smile can shift a whole moment.

Smiling is linked with the brain’s feel-good chemistry, and research into facial feedback suggests that forming a smile—even deliberately—can gently nudge our mood in a positive direction. Sometimes, the feeling follows the expression.

Smile Coffee is our little experiment in positive momentum: a brighter moment for you, a warmer exchange across the counter, and 10% off your coffee. Because good moods are worth sharing.

Private by design. Face detection and smile analysis happen entirely on this device—no remote server is involved, and live video never leaves the browser. After payment, one snapshot is sent solely as part of the printed receipt and is never stored by this app.
